## About Me

I am an ecologist interested in understanding **how and why ecosystems change across space and across time**. This work aims to help us as communities better **understand the impacts that our actions have on ecosystems**, and to better predict how ecosystem services will change in response to our actions and to climate change. I view the study of ecosystems as necessarily interdisciplinary work that considers the ecological alongside the social. 

## Research Interests

- **Adaptive management:** iterative decision making informed by monitoring and outcomes
- **Critical cartography:** map-making with the acknowledgement that maps are not neutral representations of space but reflect systems of power, typically aligned with the interests of the mapmaker
- **Data science:** detecting ecological patterns and processes through analysis 
- **Ecosystem services:** recognizing how our well-being is tied directly and indirectly to ecological processes
